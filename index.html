A few useful details

Frequently asked questions.

How do your custom AI assistants work, and are they safe?

Each assistant is configured for one clear job and uses only information you have approved. AI can make mistakes, so important outputs are reviewed by a person before use, and the assistant supports your team rather than replacing their judgement. We never ask for passwords, payment-card details or special-category personal data.

What does "fractional" mean?

It means part-time, ongoing support: a set number of hours each month instead of a permanent hire. It suits businesses that need consistent operational help without the cost and commitment of a full-time role.

How does a first engagement work?

We start with a short discovery call, then agree a written scope for a Workflow Review. From there you can choose to stop, build a project, or move to a monthly retainer.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We focus on small and growing businesses, typically owner-led teams with repeatable admin or operational processes. A first conversation helps establish whether a project is a good fit.

Do you replace our existing software?

Not by default. We first look at the tools already in use and whether a simpler process can be created around them.

Do you offer cybersecurity or financial advice?

No. M.&.CO's current focus is operational organisation and low-risk workflow support. We do not provide cybersecurity services, regulated financial advice, legal advice or specialist compliance assessments.

How do you handle business information?

Projects should use the minimum information needed, with client permission and appropriate adult supervision. We do not request passwords, payment-card details, special-category personal data or access to sensitive production systems. Enquiries may be sorted and drafted with AI tools, and a person reviews anything important.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the process, tools and agreed deliverables. We provide a written scope and fee for review before any commitment.
