A few questions

Good to know.

How long does a website take?

Timescales depend on the number of pages, content and project requirements. A realistic timeline will be agreed before work begins.

Can you work with my existing branding?

Yes. We can work with your existing logo, colours and visual identity, or discuss a separate visual direction.

Do I need to provide the website content?

You can provide your own text and images. Copywriting or additional content support can be discussed as a separate service.

Are the portfolio projects real clients?

The projects shown are self-initiated concepts demonstrating creative direction. They are not presented as commissioned client work.

Are hosting and domain names included?

Platform subscriptions and domain registration are separate unless specifically included in an agreed quote.
